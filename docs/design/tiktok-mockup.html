5 · How the data flows

StepWhat happensWhere
DetectHost is tiktok.com, vm.tiktok.com, or vt.tiktok.com.convex/ai.ts
FetchCall https://www.tiktok.com/oembed?url=…. Public. No key. Not bot-hostile. Returns caption, creator name, handle, thumbnail URL and size.convex/ai.ts
StoresiteName = "TikTok", heroImageUrl = thumbnail_url, aspectRatio = w/h, content = caption, new author = "@handle".convex/schema.ts (one optional field)
ClassifyExisting generateObject call. Input is the caption and hashtags. Output is title, tags, spaces, intents. No new prompt.convex/ai.ts
Render cardIf siteName === "TikTok": show creator handle + glyph overlay and play pill. Ratio 9:16 is inside the current clamp (0.5).item-card.tsx
Render detailIf siteName === "TikTok": show poster with play button and creator row. Hide the article reader. "Open" uses WebBrowser.openBrowserAsync. iOS hands off to the TikTok app if installed.item-detail.tsx

6 · What this design does not do

  1. No in-app video playback. The TikTok embed needs a WebView and autoplay is blocked. "Open" is one tap and always works.
  2. No transcript. oEmbed does not return it. The AI classifies from the caption. Good enough for most saves.
  3. No Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts yet. Same pattern. Add a host check when needed.
